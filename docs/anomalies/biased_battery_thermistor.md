## Root cause

The battery thermistor reads 8 K high (injected `biased_battery_thermistor`; for example a calibration or bonding error). The battery is no warmer, so the EKF's R0 is unchanged, but the nominal R0 it is compared with is computed at the wrong temperature. The thermostat would also switch on 8 K late in a cold case.

## Recommended fix

Operations: treat battery temperature as suspect and keep heater setpoints 8 K higher until it is resolved. Flight software: add a commandable thermistor offset, and cross-check the battery thermistor against the structure node and the R0 ratio. Design: fit a redundant battery thermistor.
